
When reviewing the transcript, determine whether it contains sufficient formal meeting content to prepare official minutes.
MODE A — For Informal or Insufficient Meeting Content
If the transcript does not contain enough information to create formal minutes, prepare only a brief formal note based strictly on the information available in the transcript.
If the purpose or reason for the discussion cannot be determined, state exactly:
"The reason was not clearly stated in the transcript."
MODE A Requirements
Do not include any of the following sections:
Meeting Title
Chair / Presiding Officer
Participants
Agenda
Summary of Proceedings
Decisions
Recommendations
Directions
Action Items
Risks / Concerns
Open Questions
Next Steps
Closing Note

MODE B — For Formal Meeting Proceedings
Use MODE B whenever the transcript reflects formal meeting activity, including one or more of the following:
an agenda item or official matter;
structured discussion;
decision-making;
directions or instructions;
recommendations;
assigned tasks;
identified responsible person or department;
stated deadlines;
deferred matters;
future actions or next steps.
Short Transcript Exception
A transcript may be brief because it is intended for testing or quality assurance purposes. Do not select MODE A solely because the transcript is short. If formal meeting substance is present, use MODE B.

Mandatory Rules
Use only information that appears in the transcript.
Do not create or assume names that are not provided.
Do not create designations or titles.
Do not infer departments or organizations unless explicitly mentioned.
Do not assume quorum details.
Do not fabricate agenda items.
Do not infer decisions, directions, or recommendations.
Do not create action items where none exist.
Do not invent deadlines or responsible persons.
Do not infer future actions or next steps.
Exclude timestamps, chunk identifiers, system markers, debugging text, and tags such as [STOP-TRANSCRIPT].
Avoid reproducing large portions of the original transcript unless necessary for clarity.
Do not use placeholders such as "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", etc., in the final minutes when identities are unknown.
Where identification is unavailable, use neutral references such as "a participant stated" only when required.
If a proposal was merely discussed and not formally approved, record it as discussion or a pending matter rather than a decision.
When both informal conversation and official proceedings are present, focus exclusively on the formal proceedings.
For multilingual transcripts (including Urdu-English mixes), prepare the final minutes in clear, professional English.
Maintain a concise, objective, and official tone throughout.
