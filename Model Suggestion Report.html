🏆 Overall Recommendation

Phi-4 Mini is recommended as the production model for CPU-only MoM generation on this hardware profile. It is 4.7× faster than Qwen3:4B while producing well-structured, hallucination-controlled output. Qwen3:4B produces slightly richer detail (particularly on long formal transcripts such as transcript_3.txt) but its generation times — exceeding 25 minutes for a single transcript — render it impractical for operational use on an Intel i5 8th Gen. For non-time-critical batch jobs where output quality is paramount, Qwen3:4B may still be preferred.

Key Findings at a Glance:
Phi-4 Mini completes all three transcripts in under 12 minutes total. Qwen3:4B exceeds 26 minutes on transcript_1.txt alone. Both models respect the MoM prompt structure and avoid inventing names or decisions not present in the transcript. Qwen3:4B produces more nuanced summaries on longer, formal proceedings; Phi-4 Mini is more consistent but occasionally adds cautionary meta-notes not in the original transcript.
